# Original coupon illustrations

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Style reference: user-supplied Flanders coupon. Final artwork contains no text or branding; coupon copy is rendered by the app. Two rejected portrait variants were not used.

Assets: `dist/assets/coupon-snacks-art.webp`, `dist/assets/coupon-bonus-art.webp`. The generated PNGs were resized to 900px wide and encoded as WebP for the application.

## Final prompts

Snack artwork: Create an entirely different scene from the attached reference, borrowing only its expressive oil/gouache brushwork, warm gold and charcoal palette, flowing navy and ochre folk hop ornaments. Landscape 3:2 premium snack coupon illustration: rustic dark wooden serving board holding a ceramic bowl of golden pistachios and roasted almonds, crisp rye sticks spilling across linen, airborne crumbs in golden light, three-quarter overhead. No people or beer. Food in the right 55%; left 45% calm black painted negative space. Primary subjects inside the center horizontal band for a 2.2:1 crop. No text, numbers, logos, discounts, dates, labels or UI.

Bonus artwork: Create a wholly new landscape 3:2 loyalty coupon illustration. Use only the reference's expressive oil/gouache style, golden ochre, dark forest green, navy shadows and folk hops ornament. An emerald gift box on a rustic counter, lid slightly open revealing kraft packets of gourmet nuts and crunchy snacks, generous golden satin bow flowing into botanical brushstrokes, warm gold particles. Three-quarter still life in the right half; left 45% dark green painted negative space. Compose for a 2.2:1 crop. No people, faces, coins with symbols, writing, logos, labels, badges, dates, buttons or watermark.
